Where does a city sit on the heat island spectrum?

Thirteen geographic, climate and socio-economic signals, standardized and passed through a K-Means model trained on cities, sort any location into one of four thermal profiles.

Water — cooler, humid, low heat retention
Green Space — high greenness, low AQI
Industrial — high energy use & pollution
Urban — dense, hot, low greenness
